Model Limitations & Ethical Notes
⚠️
Training distributionGains were optimised on simulated cars within defined ranges. Extrapolating outside bounds may give unreliable predictions.
🌡️
Simplified physicsThe bicycle model omits tyre non-linearity, suspension dynamics, and load transfer. Real-world deployment requires hardware validation.
🔁
Stationary modelThe model does not adapt online. If dynamics change (e.g. tyre wear), predicted gains become stale.
🚗
Safety critical contextPID gains are for research and demonstration only. Do not use in any real vehicle without exhaustive testing and regulatory sign-off.
