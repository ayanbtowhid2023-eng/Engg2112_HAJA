Model Limitations & Ethical Notes
⚠️
Training distribution Gains were optimised on simulated cars within defined ranges. Extrapolating outside these bounds (e.g. m > 4500 kg) may give unreliable predictions.
🌡️
Simplified physics The bicycle model omits tyre non-linearity, suspension dynamics, and load transfer. Real-world deployment requires hardware validation.
🔁
Stationary model The model does not adapt online. If a car's dynamics change (e.g. tyre wear), the predicted gains become stale.
🚗
Safety critical context PID gains from this tool are for research and demonstration only. Do not use in any real vehicle without exhaustive testing and regulatory sign-off.
